**Status:** complete, read-only audit

**Scope:** audit the P2.27.2 shadow-plateau FAIL using only the existing P2.27.2 output and preserved snapshots. No rerender, re-encode, production-code change, installation, commit, push, CUDA transition, or mathematical change was performed.

## 1. Executive verdict

| Question | Verdict | Evidence-qualified interpretation |

## 6. Real-plateau checks A–G

### A. Is `0.005` isolated?

**No in the post-encode output.** The rounded `0.005` bin is only 1.28208% of all decoded-MKV ROI samples, while `0.004` is 2.45912% and `0.006` is 2.54735%. In the preserved pre-encode subset, the `0.005` rounded bin is frequent, but the available raw subset range is `0.0045213359`–`0.0049789720` nits rather than one exact unrounded value.

### B. Is there a broad range of nearby values?

**Yes.** The all-120 decoded MKV has 17,354 actual float32 luminance values and 320 values after the diagnostic's three-decimal rounding. The low-end table contains substantial populations from `0.000` through `0.019` nits, including both sides of `0.005`.

### C. Is there continuity below and above `0.005`?

**Yes, with post-encode quantization gaps noted.** `0.004` and `0.006` have substantial populations, and their raw ranges meet the expected neighboring-bin structure around the `0.005` interval. The empty `0.013`, `0.017`, and `0.020` display bins prevent claiming perfect occupancy of every rounded bin, but they do not isolate `0.005`.

### D. Is there a monotonic low-end gradient?

**Numeric evidence is consistent with a broad low-end gradient, not a single shelf.** The bin populations and raw ranges span both sides of `0.005`, and the higher low-end bins generally progress through the region. Strict visual monotonicity cannot be proven from an aggregate histogram without spatial inspection.

### E. Is the number of unique luminance values high?

**Yes for the actual decoded values.** The final MKV has 17,354 actual float32 values. The diagnostic sees 320 after three-decimal rounding. The exact preserved pre-encode aggregate has 2,030 rounded values. These counts are inconsistent with a single-value low-end collapse, although they do not alone prove the absence of a spatial artifact.

### F. Do neighboring values have significant populations?

**Yes.** In the final MKV, `0.004` has 442,641 samples, `0.005` has 230,774, and `0.006` has 458,523. The rounded `0.002` bin is the largest at 620,189, further showing that `0.005` is not the unique low-end attractor in the encoded output.

### G. Does `0.005` correspond to actual black scene pixels?

**No evidence of that in the output RGB check.** For the 230,774 decoded-MKV pixels in the rounded `0.005` luminance bin:

- exact black RGB16 code count `[0, 0, 0]`: `0`
- black fraction among the `0.005` bin: `0.0`
- component-wise RGB16 minimum: `[0, 654, 0]`
- RGB16 median: `[969, 969, 969]`
- RGB16 mean: `[947.95025, 968.40588, 1067.48050]`
- component-wise RGB16 maximum: `[1784, 1388, 3583]`
- most common RGB16 triplet: `[969, 969, 969]`, `123,364` pixels (`53.45663%` of the selected bin)

For comparison, exact black `[0,0,0]` occurs 16,908 times in the entire decoded ROI, but none of those black-code pixels is in the rounded `0.005` selection. The `0.005` luminance bin therefore does not represent a black-code population in the available output check.

## 9. Interpretation of the difference

There are two separate measurements:

1. **Pre-encode P2.27.2 diagnostic:** exact preserved aggregate, 120 in-memory frames, 18,000,000 samples, rounded `0.005` at 5.134461111%, formal FAIL.
2. **Post-encode existing-MKV diagnostic:** read-only decode of all 120 output frames, 18,000,000 samples, rounded `0.002` at 3.445494444%, formal PASS; rounded `0.005` is only 1.282077778% overall and never exceeds 5% in an individual frame.

The encoded output is not a lossless copy of the pre-encode float distribution for purposes of this rounded-bin diagnostic. It must therefore not be substituted for the exact preserved P2.27.2 aggregate. Conversely, the post-encode result is strong evidence that the delivered MKV does not contain an all-120 `0.005` shadow plateau under this diagnostic.

The preserved pre-encode snapshots explain why the FAIL cannot be dismissed as a single accidental frame: frames 60 and 119 are above 5%, while frame 0 is just below at 4.953333333%. However, only three pre-encode frames were saved, so the exact all-120 pre-encode frame-by-frame cause cannot be reconstructed.
